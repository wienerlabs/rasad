# Store listing: English (en-US)

## App name

Rasad: Sky, Crescent, Qibla

## Short description

Stars, the crescent, qibla and prayer times on the real sky. Turkish interface.

## Full description

Note: Rasad's interface is in Turkish.

Rasad is a pocket observatory. Hold your phone up to the sky and every star, planet, constellation and the qibla appear exactly where they are. Everything is computed on your device; there are no accounts, ads or analytics.

The sky
• 8,920 stars, the planets, the Sun and the Moon in their real positions, with a realistic atmosphere, twilight and Milky Way.
• The Moon is lit from the Sun's real direction, with the right phase and bright limb.
• 89 constellations. A flowing path of arrows leads you to the one you search for, and the figure draws itself in when you find it.
• If a target is below the horizon, the app tells you when it rises and takes you to the best time with one tap.
• Compass mode, free navigation, a time machine and a red night vision mode.

Star names
• The Arabic names of 126 stars with transliteration, meaning and story. Names are shown for their origins only; nothing ties luck or fortune to the stars.

The crescent
• The conjunction, crescent visibility on the next three evenings by Yallop's criterion, a western horizon diagram and a world visibility map.
• Shows where and when to look for the crescent of each of the next twelve months. The month begins with the sighting; the app only tells you where and when to look.

The calendar
• Prayer times from the Sun's position at your location: Diyanet angles or Umm al-Qura, and the majority or Hanafi Asr. Every time can be opened in the sky.
• False and true dawn: see the zodiacal light in the sky and tell the two dawns apart.
• Only days with a basis in the Quran or authentic Sunnah. You can move the month start one day either way to match the local sighting.
• Qibla sun: the two moments a year when the Sun stands over the Kaaba, where the Sun is in your sky at that moment, and a shadow dial.
• Solar and lunar eclipses visible from your location in the next three years.
• The du'a for sighting the crescent when you centre a young crescent.

Sources
• Quran verses in the Tanzil text with the Diyanet translation; hadith with their Arabic text, source and grading. Texts come from verified sources.

Privacy
• The app has no internet permission. Your location is used only on your device; to show the place name, Android's address service receives your location rounded to about 2 kilometres.

Rasad is made by Wiener Labs.
